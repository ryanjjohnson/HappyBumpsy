🛡️ Safe zone · the rules

  • Score: land your bottom on someone's top. +1. Side contact just bounces.
  • Move: mouse, touch, or WASD. Last one used wins.
  • Opossum: touch it and you play dead for 5 s: upside down, half speed, can't bump. It takes 10 taps to score on you.
  • Rage possum: get scored on 3× in 10 s and you become a possum for 10 s. Anyone you touch plays dead and you get the point.
  • HONK: loiter in the bottom-right box for 15 s to release a Canada goose from the top-left. Every extra player in the box doubles the speed: two of you is 7.5 s, three is under 4. It chases for 20 s, you first. A hit in your side: 20 s playing dead and 30 s as 👁️👄👁️. Top or bottom just bounces it.
  • Greylag goose: wanders with her goslings and gets in the way. Bump a gosling and she hunts you. Caught: mint jelly for 10 s, 👁️👄👁️ for 30 s. Bump a gosling after that: exactly 67 points.
  • Nothing ever costs points. Penalties are cosmetic.
  • Safe zone: this box. No bumps for or against you, hazards ignore you. Step out to play.
